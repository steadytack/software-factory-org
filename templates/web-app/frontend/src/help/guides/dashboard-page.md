## Understanding the KPI cards {#dashboard-kpis}
<!-- keywords: kpi, dashboard, draft, surveys, stats -->

The four cards summarise live data: **draft surveys** (not yet completed), **surveys this week** with the change vs the prior week, and **total sites**. The draft-surveys card opens the matching surveys; the total-sites card opens the sites table.

## Reading the charts {#dashboard-charts}
<!-- keywords: chart, trend, category, graph -->

The line chart shows surveys per day over the last 7 days; the bar chart breaks surveys down by status. Hover for exact values.
