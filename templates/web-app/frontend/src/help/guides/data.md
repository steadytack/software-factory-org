## Working with tables {#table}
<!-- keywords: table, filter, sort, columns, csv, export, views, density, wrap, freeze, resize, bulk, select, side panel -->

Click a row to open it: Sites open in a **side panel** (with a link to the full page), Surveys open their page. Click a header to **sort**, drag its edge to **resize**, and use search and **filters** (a status filter can match several values). The bookmark saves the arrangement as a named view, and the export button downloads the filtered rows as **CSV**. The **gear** sets row spacing, text wrap and a frozen first column for every table (also under Profile). Tick rows (on a phone, press and hold a card) to **set a field** on all of them at once or delete them. In **edit mode**, click a cell to change it: Enter saves, Esc cancels.

## Record pages {#records}
<!-- keywords: record, detail, page, site, survey, history, save -->

Every site and survey has its own page: a **Details** card with its facts and links to the records above it, the main buttons in the header, and more (reopen, delete) in the **⋯** menu. Tabs hold its related records, **comments**, **attachments** and **history** (who changed what, and when). On a site's Overview you edit in place: a **Save bar** appears once you change something, and leaving with unsaved changes asks first. A deleted survey goes to the recycle bin, where an admin can restore it; sites are archived instead.

## Photos and files {#files}
<!-- keywords: photo, file, upload, attachment, camera, drag, drop, multiple, gps -->

On a record's **Attachments** tab, choose several files at once or **drag and drop** them anywhere on the tab; they upload one after another (up to 10 MB each; a larger one is skipped and named). On a phone, **Take photo** opens the camera. The map's Layers panel also accepts several GeoJSON files, dropped or chosen.

## Working offline {#offline}
<!-- keywords: offline, signal, no connection, sync, upload, waiting, outbox, phone, update, new version, reload -->

Sign in once with a signal and the app keeps working without one: it opens signed in, and every site and survey is kept on the device (refreshed every 10 minutes while online). You can add and edit records, comments and photos offline, even a new site with its surveys. They show **Waiting to upload** and are sent, in order, as soon as the connection returns. The cloud icon in the header shows what's waiting, with **Send now** and **Discard**. Anything the server refused stays there until you choose Send now or Discard it. Needs a signal first: comments, files and history of records you haven't opened, map areas you haven't viewed, search, and admin tasks. Logging out clears the records kept on the device; changes still waiting stay, and are sent the next time you sign in. When a new version of the app is out, a **New version is ready** message offers **Reload**; use it when you're at a good stopping point. If a page ever says it can't load after an update, reload the tab.
