## Maintenance actions {#admin-maintenance}
<!-- keywords: admin, reseed, orphans, maintenance, stats -->

**Re-seed demo data** runs the idempotent seeder (no-ops when sites exist). **Clean orphaned records** removes comments/attachments whose record was deleted. The **Recycle bin** restores or permanently removes deleted surveys, **Users** manages who can sign in and their roles, and **Export** downloads all the data. Changelog entries are always kept as the audit trail.
