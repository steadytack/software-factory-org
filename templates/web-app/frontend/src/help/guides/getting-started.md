## Navigating the app {#navigation}
<!-- keywords: sidebar, navigation, pages, menu, phone, tab bar, plus -->

Use the **sidebar** to switch between Dashboard, Sites, Surveys and Map; Profile and Admin sit below. The collapse button at the bottom (or `Ctrl B`) shrinks it to an icon rail. On a phone, the bottom bar holds your main pages, **More** holds the rest, and the round **+** button adds a survey or site from anywhere. Choose which pages the bar shows under Profile → Phone tab bar.

<!-- feature:share:start -->
The **share** button in the header sends a link to the page you're on (a record, a filtered table or a map view) through your device's share menu, or lets you copy or email it.
<!-- feature:share:end -->

## Global search (Ctrl+K) {#search}
<!-- keywords: search, jump to, cmd+k, shortcut, find, command, palette, theme -->

Press `Ctrl K` (or ⌘K on Mac) anywhere to open the search palette. Type to find sites and surveys, jump to a page, or run a command: **New site**, New survey, switch the theme, or (on a table page) export it as CSV. Use ↑↓ to move and ↵ to choose. Recent searches are remembered.

## Keyboard shortcuts {#shortcuts}
<!-- keywords: keyboard, shortcuts, hotkeys, keys, ? -->

Press `?` anywhere (outside a text field) for the full list. `G` then `D`, `S`, `V`, `M` or `P` jumps to Dashboard, Sites, Surveys, Map or Profile. In a table, `/` focuses the search, ↑↓ move between rows, `Enter` opens the side panel, `Shift Enter` the full page, `Space` selects, and `N` adds a record. In the side panel, ↑↓ step through records and `Esc` closes it.
