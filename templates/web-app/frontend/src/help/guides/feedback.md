## Sending feedback {#feedback}
<!-- keywords: bug, report, problem, feedback, feature, idea, suggest, support, contact -->

Open **Help & feedback** from the account menu (or the More sheet on a phone) and choose the **Feedback** tab. Pick Problem, Suggestion or Question, write a summary and the details, and send. A screenshot of the page behind the panel is included (switch it off if you'd rather not), along with details that help fix things: the page, your role, browser and device, and any recent errors. **What's sent with it** lists everything. Offline, it sends when you're back online. Admins choose where reports go under Admin → Feedback.
