## Record tabs {#detail-tabs}
<!-- keywords: tabs, overview, surveys, comments, attachments, changelog -->

A site has five tabs: **Overview** (an at-a-glance summary, then its details to edit in place), **Surveys** (every visit to the site — add, edit, filter and change status in place), **Comments**, **Attachments** (several files at once, or drag and drop; max 10 MB each) and **History** (every change and who made it). Add survey is in the header; archive and delete are in the **⋯** menu.

## Unsaved changes {#detail-dirty}
<!-- keywords: save, discard, dirty, changes -->

Changing any field on the Overview brings up a **Save bar** with **Save** and **Discard**. Discard goes back to the last saved version; leaving the page with unsaved changes asks first.
