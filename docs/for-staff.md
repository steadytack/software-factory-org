# Got an idea for an app? Start here

Organisation A has a quick, safe way to turn an idea into a working app, built with AI and checked by people at every step. You don't need to be technical. You need a problem worth solving.

## What you can ask for
Anything that would save time, cut errors or make a job easier: a form that replaces a spreadsheet, a tool that matches invoices to orders, a board for swapping shifts, a dashboard of something you count by hand today. Small is good. Small ideas get built fastest.

## How to send it
Fill in the **idea card**: five short questions, in your own words.

1. **What's the problem?** What goes wrong or takes too long today?
2. **Who does it affect?** Which team or role, and roughly how many people?
3. **How is it done today?** The steps, the tools, where it waits or gets redone.
4. **What would better look like?** How you would know it worked: a number if you have one.
5. **What information does it use?** For example rotas, orders or customer records. Say where that information lives.

Don't worry about having all the answers. "I don't know" is a fine answer. The team will work out the rest with you.

## What happens next
| Step | What happens | Roughly how long |
|---|---|---|
| **1. Assess** | The team writes your idea up as a one-page brief and checks it: is the problem real, is it worth it, is the data there, what could go wrong? | Days |
| **2. Decide** | A named person decides: **go**, **try it small first**, **get ready** (fix something first, then look again) or **stop**. You hear why. | At the decision meeting |
| **3. Try it** | Most ideas start as a **spike**: a small working version built in about a week, to see if the approach works. | About a week |
| **4. Grow it** | If the spike works, it can grow into something people use, and later into a supported app. Each step up is a decision, never an accident. | Weeks, depending on size |

## The four sizes of app
- **Spike**: a quick try to answer one question. It may be thrown away.
- **Exploration prototype**: looks and feels like the real thing, so people can react to it.
- **Business prototype**: does real work for a team, kept simple underneath.
- **Production app**: relied on, fully tested, supported and looked after.

## What keeps it safe
- Nothing goes outside the organisation, or into a live system, without a person approving it.
- Only the kinds of information the organisation allows are used. Personal or confidential data needs extra approval, or made-up test data instead.
- Every app is checked against what was asked for before anyone uses it.
