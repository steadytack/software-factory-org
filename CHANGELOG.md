# Changelog

Changes to Organisation A's factory (stages, standards, scripts, schemas, the `factory` skill). Versions follow `factory/standards/versioning.md`.

## 0.6.0 (2026-10-31)
The tiers came from a single-person factory (Explore, Personal tool, Shared pilot, Operational) and mixed two things: how mature a build is and how much protection it needs. They are now separate.
- **Four tiers by maturity** (`factory/standards/tiers.md`, new): **1 Spike** (one question, fast, throwaway), **2 Exploration prototype** (people see it and react, to shape the idea), **3 Business prototype** (real users and real data; works end to end like a production system, simpler underneath, not production-grade testing or resilience), **4 Production app** (to be relied on). Real data starts at tier 3: tiers 1 and 2 use synthetic or public data only. Moving up is a gate decision, never drift: 1 and 2 grow at their Gate 3, and 3 becomes 4 by a change request that lists what tier 4 adds (`changes.md`, "Raising the tier").
- **Protection is separate from the tier** (`security.md`): levels A to D follow the data and who can reach it (A: builder or reviewers, synthetic data; B: organisation users, Internal data; C: Confidential or personal data, or many users; D: outside users, money or regulated decisions), at every tier.
- **The spike route becomes the light route** (`stages/light.md`, replacing `spike.md`; `plan.md` replacing the spike plan; `scaffold.py plan`; `pipeline.py light`), for tiers 1 and 2: `decide <slug> go --route light --tier 1|2` ("go as a spike", "go as a prototype"), time box 1 or 2 person-weeks by default. Gate 3 ends grow (a spike to tier 2 on the light route, or either to Spec at tier 3 or 4 with `--tier`), keep (as a demonstration on synthetic data) or stop. Schemas: the decision's `route` is `full` or `light` with `tier`; the state's `route` is `light` and `spike` becomes `light` (with `tier` and `to_tier`).
- **The full route is tier 3 or 4.** DEF-01 is now tier 3, Business prototype; `check_spec.py` warns when a definition in the factory is tier 1 or 2. The spec pack scales (`specification-pack.md`): tier 3 writes in full what real use needs and keeps 04 and 05 short; tier 4 writes everything.
- **Hosted in Docker at every tier** (DEF-02), developed and tested locally in the same containers; the host is for the organisation to name. DEF-03: a login for anything reachable by others, per-user accounts from tier 3.
- `PRINCIPLES.md` (copied into each app) loses its personal-project framing and its three hardening levels for the four tiers; `testing.md` sets test depth by tier; the hardening audit is required at tier 4; the operations and security lenses activate at tier 3; the definition template defaults to tier 3 and hosted; the skill, READMEs, stages and `factory/web/` follow. The `brew-ratio` fixture is now tier 3.
- Migration: none. No idea used the 0.5.0 spike route or a tier.

## 0.5.0 (2026-10-31)
A check that the factory develops ideas rather than blocking them found four places where it would hold a good idea back or slow it. Each is fixed.
- **An unknown is amber, not red.** 0.3.1 rated a foundation red when a screening question was unanswered, and red refuses a Go, so an idea was held back for being early. Now amber covers a question not yet answered (with what would settle it), and red is kept for a known blocker: data that exists but is not usable, no owner, a worst outcome that cannot be accepted (`develop-assess.md`).
- **Go as a spike** (`factory/stages/spike.md`, template `project-files/spike.md`, `scaffold.py spike`): when the value looks real and a small build would answer the ambers faster than more research. `spike.md` (the question, success and kill criteria decided up front, the time box, the smallest scope, the data and its classes, the template, throwaway or seed) is approved with Gate 1 in place of a spec pack and an overview; `pipeline.py decide <slug> go --route spike [--timebox N]` approves gates 1 and 2 and moves the idea to Build at tier 1. Gate 3 checks the result against the criteria and the outcome, then `pipeline.py spike <slug> grow|keep|stop`: grow goes to the full Spec (gates 2 and 3 reset, starting from what the spike showed), keep goes to Publish as a tier 1 tool (`publish.py` ships `spike.md` in place of the spec), stop closes it with its reason. A red foundation is still refused, and every policy applies in full.
- **Work it through.** Develop has an owner-led working session to thrash an idea out (alternatives, failure modes, a smaller first version) before or while the brief is drafted. DEF-17's limit is on the factory's questions, not the owner's thinking (DEF-17, the lenses' question rules and the skill say so).
- **Get Ready has an owner and a review date.** `decide ... get-ready` needs `--owner` and takes `--review` (four weeks by default; a past or malformed date is refused). `ideas/pipeline.md` has a Getting ready table (fix, owner, review date) and flags one past its date as overdue; the status line counts them ("past review"). At the review date the idea comes back to Gate 1, fixed or not.
- Schemas: the assessment's `decision` gains `route`, `timebox`, `owner` and `review`; the state gains `route`, `spike` (how it ended) and the Get Ready `owner` and `review`. `status.py` shows a spike's stage as "build (spike)". The skill, `factory/README.md`, `README.md`, `spec.md` (starting from a grown spike), `audit.md`, `publish.md`, the overview template and `factory/web/` follow.
- Migration: none. No idea is at Gate 1 or beyond, and every new field is optional for existing records.

## 0.4.0 (2026-10-31)
The factory now learns from its own record, with an approver deciding every change: it records, then proposes.
- **`learned.py`** (new): `add` appends a gate answer that differed from a recommendation to `context/learned.md`; `review` lists candidate changes; `mark <key> confirmed|superseded` records the approver's answer; `outcome <slug>` writes an idea's outcome record; `check [<slug>]` validates them (with a slug, the Gate 3 check).
- **`learned.md`** gains a **Key** column (`DEF-nn`, or a short key such as `gate1-decision`) so the same question can be found across projects; the table is validated (dates, keys, scopes, statuses).
- **Outcome record at Gate 3** (`ideas/<slug>/outcome.md`, template `project-files/outcome.md`, schema `outcome.schema.json`): whether each foundation's Gate 1 rating held, effort estimated against actual, whether the one thing was the thing that mattered, and lessons naming the lens, knock-out rule, default or RICE factor they would change. `audit.md` adds it as step 6 and on the Gate 3 page.
- **Review at every gate** (the skill's gate protocol): the same override chosen in two or more projects, a foundation rated wrongly (`held: no`) in two or more builds, the median effort off by 1.5x or more, or the same lesson in two or more builds becomes a numbered decision on the gate page. On yes the entries are marked `confirmed` and the default, lens or knock-out rule is edited as a change to the factory; nothing changes by itself.
- `check_factory.py` checks `learned.md` and every outcome; `context/README.md` ("How the factory learns"), `factory/README.md`, `develop-assess.md` (Gate 1 overrides recorded with `learned.py add`) and the skill follow.
- Migration: none. `learned.md` had no entries, and no idea has reached Gate 3.

## 0.3.1 (2026-10-30)
- **Lens screening for the five foundations.** `business-value` (Problem, Value), `users-and-workflows` (People & Process), `data-and-integrations` (Data Foundations) and `security` (Risk) each carry a short **Screening** block, answered in the idea brief for every idea in Develop and Assess, whether or not the lens is activated later in Spec. A foundation is rated on its screening answers (green: all answered with evidence; amber: resting on assumptions; red: unanswered or a blocker), so a rating is evidence rather than a first impression and fewer problems surface only in Spec. `develop-assess.md`, `context/lenses/README.md` (a "Screens foundation" column) and `context/README.md` say so. Guidance only: no script, schema or check changes.

## 0.3.0 (2026-10-30)
A one-line idea cannot be assessed, so an idea is now developed before it is assessed, and assessed before anything is specified. The flow is Develop and Assess ─gate 1→ Spec ─gate 2→ Build and Audit ─gate 3→ Publish ─gate 4→ done.
- **Stage 1, Develop and Assess** (`factory/stages/develop-assess.md`) replaces Stage 0 Triage and Stage 1 Analyse (`triage.md` and `analyse.md` removed). Capture (inbox, a single idea or a supplied document; no filtering), then a 1-2 page **idea brief** per idea (`factory/project-files/idea-brief.md`: problem, outcome and success measures, today's process, data, risk, people and process, strategic fit, assumptions and unknowns; at most one question round), then an **assessment** (`factory/project-files/assessment.md`, schema `assessment.schema.json`): knock-outs, the **five foundations** (Problem, Value, Data Foundations, Risk, People & Process) each red, amber or green with a reason, RICE from the brief's evidence, and **the one thing** to know or fix before investing.
- **Gate 1 is the investment decision: Go, Get Ready or Stop.** Go moves the idea to Spec; Get Ready pauses it with the foundations to fix, then `pipeline.py reassess` opens a light re-assessment (the brief and assessment are kept; only the fixed foundations are re-rated, and the check refuses one whose reason did not change); Stop closes it with the reason. `overview.md` is now filled from the brief and the assessment (RICE, foundations, the one thing, the recommendation).
- `triage.py` becomes `pipeline.py` (`capture`, `add`, `develop`, `check`, `render`, `decide`, `reassess`), keeping the deterministic RICE scoring, ranking and POL-11 data-class checks; the register is `ideas/pipeline.json` (`IDEA-nnn`, schema `pipeline.schema.json`, replacing `triage.schema.json`) and the ranked view `ideas/pipeline.md`. Each idea gets its folder when it is developed, not when it is picked; Gate 0 is gone.
- **Spec** now opens with the solution choices that were in Analyse (tier, deployment, interfaces and design, template and runner-up, candidate assets, lenses) and the template table. `application-definition.md` is kept as a document, now written at the start of Spec and approved with the spec at Gate 2: it is the small machine-readable record the stubs, `check_spec.py` and the status page already read, so keeping it was simpler than folding it into docs 01, 02 and 10. Its problem, users and success sections moved to the brief.
- `check_spec.py --definition` becomes `--assessment`, the Gate 1 check: the brief (every section, no placeholder), the assessment (valid, no placeholder, five foundations rated, RICE, the one thing, a recommendation consistent with the ratings and knock-outs, re-assessments done) and the overview. The definition is checked with the pack at Gate 2.
- State: stage `analyse` becomes `develop`; gate `0` and the `triage` record are removed; gate 1 can be `get-ready` or `stopped`; an `assessment` summary and `versions.assessment` are added. `scaffold.py new` writes the brief and assessment stubs; `scaffold.py definition` no longer writes the overview. `status.py`, `dashboard.py`, `factory/web/`, the skill, the READMEs, the lenses, `changes.md` (which gate a change returns to), DEF-17 (one question round per stage), POL-11's wording, the versioning standard, the `brew-ratio` fixture and the tests follow.
- Migration: no ideas existed in this repository, so none needed migrating.
