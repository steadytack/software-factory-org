# The specification pack

The portable contract between the stages: a developer, another AI tool or a supplier can build from it without the factory. Markdown is canonical; diagrams are Mermaid or SVG inside the pack; links are relative. Nothing private (secrets, organisation-confidential context) goes in unless the owner says so.

`python factory/scripts/scaffold.py spec <slug>` writes the stubs below (an index and 13 documents) into `ideas/<slug>/spec/`; `python factory/scripts/check_spec.py <slug>` checks it and writes `traceability.md`. IDs and table formats: `requirement-ids.md`.

| File | Contents |
|---|---|
| `README.md` | Pack index: name, version, status, tier, deployment; each document with its purpose; source material and provenance; approvals; change history |
| `01-overview.md` | One page: what it does, why, for whom, main capabilities, success measures, scope, tier and deployment, major reuse decisions, key risks, the next gate |
| `02-product-definition.md` | Vision, objectives (OBJ), users and stakeholders, journeys (JRN), in and out of scope, constraints, non-goals, exit or retirement criteria |
| `03-functional-requirements.md` | Requirements (FR) with priority and verification; business rules (RULE) with examples; workflows and states; errors and edge cases; import/export; background behaviour |
| `04-user-experience.md` | Interface types and navigation; screens (SCR) with purpose, elements, actions and states; journeys as flows; responsive behaviour; accessibility; design system |
| `05-non-functional-requirements.md` | Measurable quality requirements (NFR): performance, reliability, usability and accessibility, maintainability, portability, observability, backup and recovery, privacy, resources and cost |
| `06-data-model.md` | Entities (DATA) with fields and keys, relationships (REL), validation, ownership and source of truth, flows, retention and deletion, migration, classification |
| `07-security-and-permissions.md` | Authentication, roles (ROLE), permissions (PERM), security requirements (SEC), secrets, privacy, audit events, threats and abuse cases, exposure, supply chain, residual risks |
| `08-user-stories.md` | Stories (US) with role, goal and value, priority, FR and EPIC; acceptance criteria (AC) including negative cases, each with a verification |
| `09-assumptions-risks-decisions.md` | Separate registers: assumptions (ASM), open questions (Q), risks (RISK), dependencies (DEP), decisions (DEC), change requests (CR) |
| `10-architecture-and-deployment.md` | Boundaries and components (CMP), stack and rationale, base template and modules, integrations (INT), data and control flows, environments, deployment category and runtime, Compose design, secrets, logging and health, backup and rollback, release workflow, cost drivers |
| `11-delivery-plan.md` | Epics (EPIC) in order with their stories, dependencies and release boundaries; tests (TEST); Definition of Done; release acceptance criteria |
| `12-component-selection-and-gaps.md` | Catalogue search (date, catalogue version); required capabilities (CAP) with candidates, the selected asset and version, the decision, compatibility, licence, adaptation, evidence and remaining work; rejected candidates; gaps |
| `13-business-process.md` | The business process: as-is and to-be, each as a Mermaid flowchart; the to-be steps (Step, Who, What happens, Relies on) each naming the FRs they rely on; what changes (steps removed, automated, changed or new, who does what before and after, the expected time or error saving). A personal tool may keep it to a few steps |
| `traceability.md` | Generated by `check_spec.py`; never edited by hand |
| `source-map.md`, `id-map.md` | Only for an imported source or a converted pack (`requirement-ids.md`) |

## Every document
Starts with a metadata table:
```
| Doc | Version | Status | Updated | Baseline |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| SPEC-03 | 0.1 | draft | 2026-10-19 | application-definition 0.1 |
```
Status is draft, in-review, approved or superseded.

## Proportionate
The pack scales with the tier (`tiers.md`). Tiers 1 and 2 have no pack: the light route's `plan.md` replaces it. A tier 3 business prototype writes in full what real use needs (01, 02, 03, 06, 07, 08, 09, 11, 13, and 12 and 10 for what it is built from and how it runs) and keeps 04 and 05 short (05: only the thresholds real use depends on, and recovery from backup); tier 4 writes every document in full, and raising a business prototype to tier 4 fills in what it kept short (`stages/changes.md`). Each document stays short where it can; a heading that does not apply says `Not applicable: <reason>` rather than being deleted, and a whole document may be just its metadata and `Not applicable: <reason>` (a static page has no `07` beyond its metadata and a line, say). A small app has one epic. Never pad a document to look complete.
