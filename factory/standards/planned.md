# Planned stacks (no template yet)

System types the factory has no starter for. Stage 1 adds an entry here when an idea needs one ("none fits"); an entry seen twice becomes a template. When a template is built, move its entry to `factory/standards/stacks/<name>.md`.

Format: `date | idea | what was needed | nearest template used | seen N times`.

Command-line tools and libraries are deliberately not offered as a template, so an idea of that shape lands here.
