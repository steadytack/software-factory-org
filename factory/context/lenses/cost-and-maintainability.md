# Lens: cost, licensing and maintainability

**Activate:** when the app depends on outside services, paid tiers, keys, or third-party assets.

- Which outside services, libraries or keys does it need? What do they cost, what are their limits, and what happens if one disappears?
- What licence does each dependency or imported asset carry, and is it compatible with the app's licence (DEF-13)?
- What will need updating over time (dependencies, data, keys), and who does it?
- Is anything built here worth offering to the catalogue afterwards?

**Feeds:** `10-architecture-and-deployment.md` (cost drivers), `12-component-selection-and-gaps.md` (licence status, reusable work).
