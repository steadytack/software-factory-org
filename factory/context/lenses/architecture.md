# Lens: architecture and technology

**Activate:** when the app needs a server, more than one component, or a template and modules from the catalogue.

- Which deployment category fits: simple/self-contained, local (Docker Compose), hosted/shared, automated/background? Do development and deployment differ?
- Which interface types: web, mobile, specialist visual (map, timeline, chart), conversational, non-visual (API, CLI, job, agent)?
- Which approved catalogue assets fit, and what is left as a gap (reuse, adapt, build, import, redesign or defer)?
- Where are the boundaries between components, and what crosses them?
- What is the simplest architecture that meets the requirements (NFR-015 of the factory spec)?
