# Lens: business value and outcomes

**Activate:** always.

- What problem does it solve, for whom, and what happens today without it?
- What outcome shows it worked? How would the owner notice (a measure, a habit, time saved)?
- What is the smallest first release that delivers that outcome?
- What is explicitly out of scope? What is a nice-to-have in disguise?
- When would it be retired or replaced (an exit or retirement criterion), if ever?

**Screening (Develop and Assess, every idea):** for the **Problem** and **Value** foundations.
- Problem: can the problem be said in one sentence without naming a solution? Who has it, how often, and what does it cost today?
- Problem: is it one problem, or several that would be better as separate ideas?
- Value: what number would show it worked (time, errors, money, risk), and what is the baseline today?
- Value: what is the outcome worth, roughly, against the effort? Who would say so?
