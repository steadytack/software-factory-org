# Lens: users, roles and workflows

**Activate:** always.

- Who uses it, how often, on what device and where (desk, field, phone)?
- What are the three to five things they do, start to finish? Which one matters most?
- What does a first-time user see? What would make them stop using it?
- Are there different roles? Who may do what?
- What happens on a blank, huge, duplicated or malformed input, or when two things happen at once?

**Screening (Develop and Assess, every idea):** for the **People & Process** foundation.
- Who owns it once it is live: a named role that has agreed, or someone assumed?
- Who would use it, and do they want to? What would they stop doing, and who loses something?
- Where does it fit in today's process: which steps change, and which hand-offs move to someone else?
- Who has the time to take part in the spec and testing?
